About Us

Introduction about GAC

Guangzhou Automobile Group Co., Ltd. (GAC Group) was founded in June 1997 and is headquartered in Guangzhou. As of 2023, it ranks among the world's top 500 enterprises, successfully expanding to 40+ countries across the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Eastern Europe, Africa, and the Americas.

GAC is devoted to the production and sales of world-class vehicles, engines, auto parts, and accessories, as well as R&D of automobile engineering technology. Since its establishment, GAC has adopted a global vision and adhered to international standards, forming a world-leading manufacturing system encompassing GAC global production procedures, world-class production processes, and global supply chain systems.

GAC has nearly 30 years of experience collaborating with major brands in the automotive industry. Through this collaboration, GAC developed assembly plants in China, continuously enhancing product quality and improving production processes while expanding its product portfolio.

About GAC in Bangladesh

GAC entered the Bangladeshi market through its official distributor, DHS Motors Limited, bringing global automotive innovation, intelligent technology, and premium mobility solutions to local customers. Since its debut, GAC Bangladesh has introduced advanced models such as the EMZOOM, EMKOO, E9 PHEV, AION V, and AION Y, combining bold design, smart features, everyday practicality, and electric mobility for modern Bangladeshi drivers.

Backed by DHS Motors' growing sales and aftersales network, GAC Bangladesh is committed to delivering vehicles that suit the evolving lifestyles of Bangladeshi customers. With strong warranty support, customer-focused services, and a future-ready line-up of SUVs, MPVs, and electric vehicles, GAC continues to shape a smarter and more connected driving experience in Bangladesh.

With a focus on quality, innovation, safety, and reliability, GAC Bangladesh aims to redefine the future of mobility and establish itself as one of the country's leading modern automotive brands.
