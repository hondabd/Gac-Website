Lounge-Class Rear Comfort

Inside Aion Y, the rear cabin offers lounge-class comfort with exceptional legroom, a fully flat floor, and flexible seating, creating a spacious, open environment designed for effortless relaxation on every drive.

14.6" Touchscreen Display

Equipped with a 14.6-inch touchscreen, the Aion Y integrates infotainment, navigation, Apple CarPlay, and voice control into a unified system, delivering responsive interaction, high-resolution visuals, and seamless connectivity within an intelligent digital cockpit.

Aion Y features the Magazine Battery with advanced thermal management and multi-layer cell protection, engineered to resist heat propagation and maintain structural integrity under extreme conditions, ensuring enhanced safety and long-term battery durability.
